3.1 EP vs MP comparison
Eyebrow: TECHNICAL COMPARISON
H2: Electropolished (EP) vs Mechanically Polished (MP) Stainless Steel Pipe
Intro line: Electropolishing removes a thin surface layer electrochemically, levelling peaks and leaving a chromium-enriched passive film. Mechanical polishing only abrades the surface. If your specification says "electropolished", a mirror-polished pipe does not meet it.
Surface Property
	Electropolished (EP)
	Mechanically Polished (MP)
	Why It Matters
	Internal Ra (ASME BPE)
	SF4 ≤ 0.38 µm (15 µin) · SF5 ≤ 0.51 µm (20 µin) · SF6 ≤ 0.64 µm (25 µin)
	SF1 ≤ 0.51 µm (20 µin) · SF2 ≤ 0.64 µm (25 µin) · SF3 ≤ 0.76 µm (30 µin)
	Lower Ra resists bacterial adhesion and biofilm in sanitary loops
	Surface structure
	Levelled, no directional lay
	Directional abrasive scratches
	Faster, more reliable CIP and SIP cycles
	Passive layer
	Chromium-enriched [add measured Cr/Fe ratio only if you hold an XPS/AES test report]
	Standard passive layer unless separately passivated
	Better resistance to rouging and pitting
	Typical specification
	ASTM A270 + ASME BPE SF4–SF6
	ASTM A270 + ASME BPE SF1–SF3
	Commonly specified for EDE-licensed (UAE), EU-GMP and FDA-regulated product-contact systems
